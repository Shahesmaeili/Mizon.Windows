# Artist spotlight sample models

These are 3D demonstration assets, including an AI-assisted reconstruction of Moein and third-party scans. The application's demo profile names, statistics and songs are fictional; replacing a model does not turn that sample data into a real artist catalog.

The third-party scans listed below are licensed **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**. Their public Sketchfab API metadata and the GLB embedded asset metadata identify the original creators and license. The public [Allen Institute Objaverse dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse) is used as their download mirror. **MoeinBust.glb has a separate CC BY-SA 4.0 license**, described in [MOEIN-ATTRIBUTION.md](MOEIN-ATTRIBUTION.md).

The current spotlight uses **MoeinBust**, **AYA2** and **Kana**. Sam and the older stylized models below are retained as assets but are no longer selected by the spotlight.
